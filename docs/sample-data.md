# Preview data

The screenshots use a 104-photo toy library: five photos for each of twenty CelebA identities, plus four document images. Eighteen identities are confirmed; two remain as review proposals.

CelebA is available for non-commercial research purposes only. The source images are not committed and are not redistributed. The screenshots blur the faces. CelebA identity labels are numeric; the dataset does not ship names.

The receipt, intake form, and code screenshot are synthetic images generated for the demo. The whiteboard is [Change management whiteboard.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Change_management_whiteboard.jpg) by Birkenkrahe, licensed [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Dataset: [CelebA](https://mmlab.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/projects/CelebA.html). Preview mirror: [flwrlabs/celeba](https://huggingface.co/datasets/flwrlabs/celeba).
